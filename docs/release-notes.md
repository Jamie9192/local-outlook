Local Outlook is an early family preview for macOS and Windows.

- UK postcode search with configurable radius and date range.
- Local events, cinema dates, school holidays, bank holidays and weather.
- Agenda and calendar with individual detail popups.
- On-demand, cached provider requests and secure local API credentials.
- App updates in Settings: check, view release notes, download and install, then restart.

Mac: download the universal DMG (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer),
open it and drag Local Outlook into Applications. Windows: download the setup EXE.
Packaged users do not need Rust or Node.js. Configure your own data sources in Settings.

These preview builds are not Apple-notarised or Windows publisher-signed.
Your operating system may warn or block them at first launch. Update-package
signatures protect the in-app update mechanism; they are separate from Apple
and Microsoft publisher signing.

No feed covers every local event. Film dates do not confirm a local cinema
screening; review the source evidence and coverage notes before planning.
